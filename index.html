01 — The Coast of the Gods

Where the cliffs meet the Tyrrhenian.

Zambrone sits on the Costa degli Dei, the stretch of Calabrian coast between Pizzo and Capo Vaticano. White cliffs, clear water, Tropea a few minutes away and, on the horizon, the seven Aeolian Islands.

The territory · Tropea and Stromboli

Two landmarks on the horizon.

The resort looks out on the two images that define this coast: the cliff-top town of Tropea to the south-west and the volcano of Stromboli across the water.

Tropea

≈ 9 km from the resort

The Pearl of the Tyrrhenian: an old town built on the edge of a sandstone cliff, white beaches below and the sanctuary of Santa Maria dell’Isola on its rock. In December and January the Borgo Incantato festival lights the historic centre.

Stromboli

In view from the site

One of the seven Aeolian Islands, a UNESCO World Heritage site, and an active volcano. From the terraces the sun sets behind its cone. In summer, boats for the Aeolian Islands leave from the port of Vibo Marina.

Calabrian food and wine

A kitchen that starts from the land.

The restaurants draw on what grows and is caught within a few kilometres: the red onion of Tropea, fresh Tyrrhenian fish, local olive oils and the wines of Calabria.

  • Red onion of Tropea (IGP)
  • ’Nduja of Spilinga
  • Pecorino of Monte Poro
  • Swordfish and Tyrrhenian fish
  • Calabrian chilli and bergamot
  • Cirò and the wines of Calabria

02 — Architecture and masterplan

Curved terraces, one continuous gesture.

The masterplan by architect Natale De Luca rejects high-density blocks in favour of a two-storey profile that follows the slope. Pale façades, continuous horizontal lines and planted roofs leave the landscape as the unifying element.

03 — Arrival

The stay begins at the gate.

A long driveway from the coastal road leads through security gates to valet parking and a 500 sqm reception and concierge. Three separate check-ins (hotel, events, villas) and a private helipad keep every arrival discreet.
